# Evidence-qualified CYP3A4/5 molecular phenotype -> observed PK: source search (19 Oct 2026)

## Non-negotiable scientific question

For the **same tacrolimus patient, same PK target, same prediction landmark and same held-out patients**, does molecular phenotype (single omic or multiomic; direct values, biologically constrained score, latent factor, regularized model, XGBoost or graph) improve prediction beyond an appropriately adjusted **clinical + authentic CYP3A4/5 pharmacogenetic reference**? Genotype-to-CYP-activity associations **alone** cannot prove improvement of systemic tacrolimus PK. Read this as a preregistration *plan*, not evidence already established.

The author has access to MIMIC-IV and expects to request authorized BIOMARGIN ↔ ABIS clinical-PK linkage in ~2 weeks. This note documents what existing studies already support and what data must be requested. It does **not** include unpublished individual records.

## Public multiomics and ICU sources: use only for their valid tasks

- [MIMIC-IV v3.1](https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/3.1/): real linked prescription/administration, clinical labs, ICU records and possibly sparse measured drug TDM, **no released original patient-linked RNA, protein or metabolomics matrix in base schema**. Clinical PopPK/TDM model quality verification only. Need satisfy PhysioNet credential/license; NEVER publish a deidentified MIMIC patient sample table in public Actions.
- [EATRIS-Plus reference n125, Zenodo 17514796](https://zenodo.org/records/17514796): real open RNA, proteomics, metabolomics, lipids across same people, suitable for MultiOmics fusion and reference normalization **without any confirmed measured clinical drug PK**; cannot join by unrelated identifiers. Some fuller phenotype/whole-genome data are restricted via ClinData.
- [Rotroff 2016 metformin n32](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2016.00135): genuine publicly downloadable 384 pre-dose GC-TOF features and measured patient AUC/CLr with authentic same-ID crosswalk; executed Omics2PK baseline versus +3 PCA performance **no primary incremental benefit**. Different transporter/renal drug and no verified RNA SWEET substrate.
- [Silveira / Duarte 2021 rosuvastatin n40](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2021.752960), [MTBLS3128](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS3128): real predose serum metabolome and PK in study, but original published raw repository not verified to include processed same-person PK matching and author processed CSV is on access-controlled Google Drive; not a valid unverified cohort regression source.

## Minimum data request BEFORE BIOMARGIN↔ABIS

Ask the cohort team first for a **privacy-preserving, aggregate-only availability and join audit**, with subject counts (not IDs) and overlap of:

1. Real patient-by-sample crosswalk (BIOMARGIN/ABIS original pseudonym keys), original collection date/time relative to dose or visit, specimen material and batch, longitudinal subject independence.
2. Availability of **true measured** CYP3A4/5 full haplotype/alleles (donor AND recipient for liver graft, patient for kidney graft), assay technology, copy numbers/ancestry coverage, CYP3A4*22, CYP3A5*3/*6/*7 when appropriate, do NOT impute missing calls as non-expresser.
3. Original individual normalized and/or raw RNA, proteomics CYP3A4/CYP3A5/ABCB1, metabolomics pathway features (including known bile/steroid 4beta-OHC plus cholesterol if genuinely observed), technical replicates/QC, biological source (blood, liver or gut); **transcriptomic RNA not guaranteed from metabolomics**.
4. **Original tacrolimus dose administration history + formulation (IR/PR/Envarsus), C(t) with exact offsets, C0 blood whole-blood units, any dense 0-12/24h AUC, hematocrit, steroids, co-medications and liver/kidney function**, not just a patient average dose.
5. Registry/study permission to run original patient-level exploratory research **off public GitHub**, consent/governance and legal join conditions.
6. Control of sampling: exclude or stratify initial post-transplant and stable states; distinguish pre-dose for **future landmark prediction** vs treatment-conditioned biomarker for a longitudinal time-varying model, and verify current drugs/sampling precede target measurement.
7. Provide n eligible exact complete people for each analytical arm and cohort split (discovery/external) rather than assuming RNA+protein+metabolites+PK all exist on the same individuals.

**Initial minimum viable cohort:** with measured clinical+PGx+true tac trough/dosing + at least one same-subject pre-landmark omic, can test small predeclared score vs reference. For full transcriptomic SWEET/multiomic XGB/Pillar-A graph comparative study, require enough **matched independent people** and donor-heldout/external center validation to avoid p≫n overfitting. Do not claim validation on n13 or n29 simply by adding folds or synthetic augmentation.

## Preregistered ablation design for matching patients

- Same measured tac PK outcome and same donor-heldout folds for C0 clinical, C1 clinical+verified genotype, C2 C1+single-omic mechanistic score, C3 C1+one omic direct features + ridge/elastic net, C4 C1+XGB on prespecified low-dim molecular features, C5 C1+true multiomics score/MOFA (if matched modalities), C6 C1+RNA SWEET features (if actual RNA) with/without Pillar A, C7 C1+PopPK covariate at original concentration-time likelihood.
- Harmonize total donor n/complete-case intersection across each paired arm; later analyze systematic missingness. Train-only fitting of imputation, scaling, pathways, score normalization, SWEET reference, MOFA, hyperparameters and model selection. No patient repeated/timepoint leakage.
- Prioritize fixed low-dimensional pathway scores and candidate drug-metabolism/absorption/drug distribution when n small. XGBoost, MOFA and graphs become informative only on adequately sized cohorts; avoid declaring 'winner' using one LOPO score from n13.
- Prespecify delta held-out log-AUC/CL/F error, relative dose-normalized trough error for sparse TDM where appropriate, RMSE, bias/P20, calibration and subgroup shift; add uncertainty and shuffled negative controls. For actual PK mechanistic claims, use real NLME original dose–C(t) not a regressor on precomputed same-patient PK fitted on whole cohort.
- Distinguish a CYP enzyme marker from a *patient systemic tac CL/F* driver: gut first pass, ABCB1, blood partition and time post transplant may override a hepatic CYP3A4 marker. 4beta-OHC is **not guaranteed** useful for tac early transplantation; existing large negative evidence must be retained.

## BIOMARGIN/ROCKET source capacity before ABIS linkage

A direct 2025 BIOMARGIN clinical pipeline publication (PMID 40315594, DOI [10.1016/j.jpba.2025.116911](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpba.2025.116911)) documents **>1,300 enrolled transplant patients, >2,500 biopsies, ~4,200 plasma and ~9,700 urine samples** across a longitudinal multicentre clinical design. This is a large *parent project inventory*, NOT evidence that all these specimens have multiomics, original CYP3A4/5 PGx or independently measured tac C(t).

The BIOMARGIN-linked ROCKET [2025 multi-omic factor study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0085253825005125) mentions **131 individuals** with paired blood and urine collected at the time of biopsy across centers. This is a potentially suitable biological unit for external site-heldout molecular-fusion methodology, but the original participant-wise tacrolimus dosing, CYP alleles and drug TDM linkage are **not independently demonstrated**. In kidney recipients, **peripheral blood transcriptome** can track inflammatory state without directly measuring **hepatic/intestinal CYP3A4/5**, so direct claims of functional CYP expression from blood are prohibited. Consider immune/inflammation score, measured plasma metabolome, and direct genotypes as *distinct* hypotheses.

**First BIOMARGIN/ABIS meeting:** do not ask immediately for every raw archive. Obtain a study/data-steward-reported **intersection count table only**, stratified by centre, transplant stage (early vs stable), molecular modality and actual tac PK availability. A matched n in the same study is not an actual patient-level join until original pseudonym mapping, sample timing and legal grounds are independently verified. Restrict private source transfer to approved local institutional infrastructure.

Suggested three-tier minimal source release, after authorized agreement:

- Tier 1: aggregate table of n exact unique matched people/sample-visits by centre, modality and drug, missingness/batch plus legal/consent feasibility (no patient IDs);
- Tier 2: authorized original pseudonymous person+assay+dose+concentration and **one molecular layer** for fixed mechanistic score-versus-PGx proof, retaining original drug units/time and hematocrit;
- Tier 3: transcriptome+proteome+metabolome matched longitudinal source, plus centre-stratified independent replication cohort to compare graph SWEET, XGBoost and factor fusion. Not assume Tier 3 exists.

## Concrete OPEN BIOMARGIN molecular data and original-author MOFA2 code discovered (19 Oct 2026)

A major preparatory asset can be used **NOW** without awaiting the BIOMARGIN↔ABIS legal clinical linkage:

- **Original author analysis code, free public repository:** [InsermRightLab/MOFA2-Tinel-et-al](https://github.com/InsermRightLab/MOFA2-Tinel-et-al), comprising `Notebook_MOFA2_model_training.ipynb` and `Notebook_MOFA2_downstream_analysis.ipynb`. The full training notebook contains the six exact originally named processed input matrix files, their MOFA2 R recipe and a published deterministic **131-person `samples.order` join list**. Treat this code as a protocol/source reference, never as a real matched TAC dose/concentration table.
- **Blood RNAseq**, GEO [GSE175718](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE175718): genuine 384 blood biopsy-time observations from four centers, freely available **5.5 MB `GSE175718_counts.csv.gz`** plus SRA; the 131-person MOFA2 subset must be selected by original authenticated sample identity, NOT by row position or assuming all 384 are the 131.
- **Blood and allograft-biopsy mRNA microarrays**, GEO [GSE129166](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE129166): original 117 blood/95 biopsy expression profiles, original processed sample data and CEL archive. Cohort/assay overlaps need explicit original sample keys.
- **Blood microRNA**, ArrayExpress/BioStudies `E-MTAB-9595`; **biopsy microRNA**, GEO `GSE179772`; **urine RT-qPCR 34-gene view**, [Figshare 28248533](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28248533) per official author README.
- Some associated early manuscript documentation cites `GSE147089` as biopsy-derived mRNA rather than `GSE129166`, which the official MOFA2 author README labels for both blood and biopsy microarray views. **Independently verify source GSM/BIOS specimen crosswalk and tissue before any reproduction; do not silently interchange accessions.**
- These **six MOFA2 views are blood/biopsy/urine mRNA and miRNA, not six classes including proteomics and metabolomics**. They can test cross-compartment RNA-factor reproducibility and experimental network inference but **cannot** supply true multi-omics protein/metabolite incremental tac PK prediction merely by virtue of a six-view factorization.
- The official GEO study pages checked do **not** claim original same-person numeric tacrolimus troughs, exact dose/timing/formulation or CYP3A5 genotypes in the public uploaded matrices. No eligible public original tac PK joined table can be inferred from the published `samples.order`. GEO's RNAseq blood biopsy snapshots reflect immune injury and not direct liver CYP3A expression/abundance.

**Immediate privacy-preserving technical pre-BIOMARGIN plan:** reproduce the original 131-person MOFA2 input crosswalk with public RNA/miRNA source matrices, compare RNA-only single-view factors and cross-compartment factors (no PK), and separately prepare clean MultiOmics normalized individual expression export for future Omics2PK inductive SWEET. Log source checksums, row/sample matrix shape and missingness, and never treat all original sample rows as independent people. Once the legally authorized ABIS PK join becomes available, reuse verified original molecular matrices and perform **a new train-fold-only fit/projection** with real patient-held-out TAC clinical+PGx PK; do not fit all-person MOFA2 once and pass full-cohort factors into held-out PK evaluation.

**Importantly, software readiness on these open omics is not evidence that the omics phenotype improves TAC PK.** The first proof of the latter still requires authentic observed PK for the *same individuals*.
