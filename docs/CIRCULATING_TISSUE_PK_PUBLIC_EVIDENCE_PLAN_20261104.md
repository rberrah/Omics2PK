# Blood-derived molecular phenotypes versus true drug PK: public source triage (2026-11-04)

**Research hypothesis co-developed with Pierre Marquet:** Can an easily accessible circulating molecular phenotype explain **out-of-sample individual variation in measured human drug PK** beyond genotype, clinical covariates and a prespecified existing PopPK model?

**Tissue is not just a covariate: it is a validity contract.** Record `source_tissue`, `source_cell_type_or_bulk`, `sample_compartment`, `assay_platform`, `drug_related_target_organ`, `collection_before_or_after_drug`, `same_subject_crosswalk`, `measured_PK_endpoint` and `source_level_evidence` for each dataset. The tissue of an assayed molecular feature does not magically become liver or intestine because its gene is metabolically relevant. An omic measurement obtained *after exposure* must not be used to predict that earlier exposure without explicitly studying longitudinal feedback.

## Strict analysis plan — each hypothesis uses a different source

1. **Tissue validity** — GTEx matched donor bulk whole blood vs liver CYP3A4/5 and cell-composition-aware pathways. RNA gene totals and splice-specific variants are separate; liver, intestinal tissue, PBMC, plasma cell-free EV and whole blood remain distinct sources. Evaluate cross-tissue correlation *within the same donor*, not simply difference in tissue mean; no patient PK inferred.
2. **Measured liver function in vitro** — existing HLC/EPA and independently acquired Zhang/PXD lines, compare baseline PGx/clinical available to RNA, protein, activity. Probe substrate specificity and assay material (microsomes, S9, hepatocytes) recorded.
3. **Circulating signal → real individual PK in vivo** — **ALREADY EXECUTED** original Rotroff 2016 with 32 unique human PK-linked predose molecular profiles, 32 leave-one-person-out fits, no confirmed gain over age/sex/weight. Prioritize **source acquisition MTBLS3128** (rosuvastatin, n40) to replicate with a different drug and richer clinical/PGx covariates; never claim that Rotroff contains genotypes or an externally tested PopPK. Compare clinical/dose and *genotype when present* with blood metabolite pathways and unknown features. No genotype baseline invented; no use of a post-dose biomarker to predict earlier Cmax.
4. **Mechanistic in vivo external bridge** — Wegler n54 observed rosuvastatin curves + same-patient hepatic transporter proteins. Show that tissue-targeted protein helps drug-specific PK mechanistically; this does **not** mean peripheral blood is a functional proxy.

### Endpoints and testing

Primary: change in **held-out subject-level AUC or CL/F prediction**, ideally dose-normalized without post-outcome leakage, versus existing published PopPK baseline plus clinical covariates and full actual PGx if genuinely measured. Secondary: Cmax, C0, drug-specific observed metabolite formation/probe activity. Same subject folds, fold-frozen pathway selection, dose-relative sample-time audit, formal source compartment metadata, descriptive uncertainty; external replication where actual data permit.

**Critical safety gates:** `ABSTAIN_NO_TRUE_PK`, `ABSTAIN_UNVERIFIED_INDIVIDUAL_SOURCE_ROWS`, `ABSTAIN_MISSING_TISSUE_SOURCE`, `ABSTAIN_POSTDOSE_PREDICTOR_LEAKAGE`, `ABSTAIN_LIVER_BLOOD_PROXY_UNVALIDATED`, `ABSTAIN_UNPAIRED_STUDIES`. The source-provenance module in MultiOmics is tracked in [issue #119](https://github.com/rberrah/MultiOmics/issues/119).

**Do not claim** that evidence showing CYP3A gene expression in human liver can automatically be transported to a patient's whole-blood transcriptome, or that a model trained on cross-study omics proves an improvement in individual human PopPK.
