# Jarmusch real human CYP3A PGx vs pre-dose MS1: nested validation (2026-11-06)

**Executed on original public source:** [original-data successful run 42128903787](https://github.com/rberrah/Omics2PK/actions/runs/42128903787); [Jarmusch 2020 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/cts.12785). Original GNPS task `b3966445abad4658a3cdc63c8198a666`: plasma at day1 time0 **before drug**, actual 13 distinct volunteer sample IDs, categorical CYP3A4/CYP3A5 genotype, original **body weight + sex**, 6349 MS1 ion features, and genuinely observed midazolam oral CL/F n13 day1 / n12 day9. No subject rows or original clinical values exported. **Source CL/F physical units not independently qualified.**

Original public ZIP is regenerated by GNPS POST, changing the outer SHA. Three original inner files are pinned independently: params XML SHA256 `698c0f01e84f87587919d6d4021fafc12160919bdb3d2e244340eef0c579358a`; metadata TSV `5014be9e82f2edef460e306a49f7926d799d4e0811fc12d191581c6fbd045d62`; QIIME2 QZA `6f39918b9186fcecbe9fa5a5190f73f9b3e581cf2838fdaac6f13b613f3c6bd3`. Assay and subject schema also verified. Source processing was upstream of outer CV, limiting full inductive inference.

**Method**: outer leave-one-*person*-out, fourfold inner train-only choice of Ridge alpha 1/20/100 and k=0/1/3 molecular features; feature ranking, prevalence screening, scaling, genotype encoding and model tuning inside training folds. Omics models can choose **k=0**. 207 candidate ions have good *putative* GNPS MS2 matches, 18 annotated as potential steroid/bile-related ions. These are **not** authenticated endogenous molecules, analytical standards, CYP3A-selective functional phenotypes, or distinct omic platforms. The full candidate pool is 6349 untargeted MS1 features.

MAE in **original CL/F numeric units**; lower better:

| Actual midazolam PK | n | Weight+sex+CYP3A4/5 PGx | +18 putative steroid/bile ions | +207 MS2 annotated | +6349 untargeted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Day1 oral CL/F | 13 | 6.195090 | **6.195090** | 6.195090 | 6.612286 |
| Day9 oral CL/F | 12 | 3.310359 | **3.252587** | 3.780407 | 3.675309 |

At day1 the targeted 18-ion arm and 207-annotated-ion arm **choose zero ions for all 13 outer subjects**. Day9 the 18-ion arm chooses zero for 9/12 and one for 3/12, decreasing original MAE only **0.057772** (1.75% vs PGx), with uncorrected descriptive paired-loss bootstrap interval **[-0.116065,0]**. This is **not confirmatory** (n12, nested model selection, multiple arms, exploratory outcome, bootstrap does not refit models). Day9 the 207-ion arm **worsens MAE +0.470048** and the untargeted 6349-ion arm **worsens +0.364950**. The 6349-ion arm worsens day1 +0.417196 despite tuning.

**Conclusion:** no robust evidence that pre-dose plasma MS1 improves observed human midazolam CL/F over the source's limited measured PGx, even with nested biologically restricted candidate sets and regularization. This does **not** show true endogenous CYP3A phenotyping cannot work, nor establish anything on tacrolimus. Small cohort, uncertain omic identity, partial genetic panel, no external validation.
